Nullgen Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Nullgen is a browser extension that filters AI-related websites and on-page content according to rules you control. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled.

Data we collect

Nullgen does not sell user data, run ads, or send browsing history to a Nullgen server. There is no Nullgen account and no remote analytics backend operated by us for this extension.

The extension stores the following locally in your browser (Chrome storage):

  • Your settings (enabled categories, block lists, appearance preferences, allowlist)
  • Local counters for blocked items (elements, text, navigations)

Permissions

  • storage — save your settings and stats on this device (and optional sync of a small settings mirror if Chrome Sync is enabled).
  • declarativeNetRequest — block or redirect navigation to AI platforms you have enabled in your rules.
  • tabs — support “Allow this site” and popup actions for the active tab.
  • Host access (<all_urls>) — scan page content so cosmetic filters, keywords, and AI-text heuristics can run on websites you visit.

How page data is used

Content scripts may read visible text and element structure in the active page only to decide whether to hide or label matching AI-related content. That page content is processed on your device and is not uploaded to Nullgen servers.

Sharing

We do not share extension data with third parties. If you use Chrome Sync, Google may sync the small settings mirror according to Google’s own policies for Chrome Sync.

Children

Nullgen is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the extension if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the extension’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the Nullgen GitHub repository.
