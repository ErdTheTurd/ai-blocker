Detailed description:
Nullgen filters AI noise from the web the way an ad blocker filters ads — while still letting you use AI tools when you choose.

• Hide AI videos on YouTube — home, search, related, Shorts, and watch pages
• Block creators/channels you list on YouTube, X, and Twitch
• Cosmetic filters for AI badges, sidebars, and embeds
• Keyword and AI-text heuristics for generated copy
• AI image CDN / alt-text matching
• Optional hard-block of AI websites (off by default so Claude/ChatGPT still work)
• Fully customizable appearance and rule lists
• Allowlist any site in one click
• Import / export your configuration

All filtering runs in your browser. Nullgen does not sell your data.
